# Play against an engine

## Premoves

You can premove against an engine, as on Lichess.

- **Queue a move:** while the engine thinks, drag or click a piece. Its squares turn blue.
- **When it plays:** the moment the engine replies, if it is still legal. Otherwise it is dropped.
- **Cancel:** right-click, or click an empty square.
- **Promotions** become queens.

To turn premoves off, untick **Allow premoves** before starting a game.

## Chess960

Choose **Chess960** under **Start from**. Enter a position number from 0 to 959, or click
**Random**. The back rank is shown before you start; 518 is the usual chess starting position.
Fast remembers the last number. Choose an engine that supports Chess960.

Castle by moving the king onto its rook. You can also use the king's final square when
that cannot be confused with an ordinary king move. If the king already starts on its
castled square, move it onto the rook.

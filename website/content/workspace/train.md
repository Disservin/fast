# Repertoire training

Train drills your repertoire with spaced repetition.

## Line status

| Status       | Meaning              |
| ------------ | -------------------- |
| **New**      | Not practised yet    |
| **Learning** | Being learned        |
| **Due**      | Ready to be repeated |
| **Known**    | Learned; not due yet |

## Grade your moves

After each move, grade it **Again**, **Hard**, **Good** or **Easy**. Each button shows when the
move will come back.

## Leaks from your games

**Leaks from your games** lists where your own games left the repertoire, and how you scored
compared with the prepared move. **Drill these lines** practises just those lines.

# Workspace tabs

## Open tabs

Click **+** in the tab bar to choose a view: Analyse, Play, Database or Tournament. Each tab is
independent and keeps its own position and game.

New Analyse tabs also open when you:

- open a database game, or
- analyse an engine game.

Train and Settings each have a single shared tab.

## What runs in the background

- Analysis runs only in the selected tab.
- Games in other Play tabs keep their clocks running.
- Every open game saves automatically and is back in its tab after a restart.

## Tab shortcuts

`Ctrl` is `Cmd` on macOS.

| Key                                            | Action                                                       |
| ---------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------ |
| `Ctrl+T`                                       | New tab of the current type (Analyse from Train or Settings) |
| `Ctrl+Tab` / `Ctrl+Shift+Tab`                  | Next / previous tab                                          |
| `Ctrl+W`                                       | Close tab                                                    |
| `Ctrl+Shift+T` or **Reopen**                   | Restore the last closed tab (up to ten are kept)             |
| `Alt+←` / `Alt+→` (`Cmd+[` / `Cmd+]` on macOS) | Back / forward through visited tabs, like a browser          |

The back and forward buttons on a mouse work too. Closing a Play tab pauses its game, and
reopening it resumes the game.

## Check highlight

A king in check is highlighted in red on every board, including at checkmate. The highlight
clears when the check ends and is hidden while you edit a position.
